# Motion spec

- Trees grow between authored stages: each instance's crown radius, height and trunk ease toward the species curve at the chosen year, switching between three normalised stage meshes with continuous scale.
- Shade is a map: it expands on the ground as trees grow, as the month fills the crowns with leaves and as the hour moves the sun.
- The timeline moves season, canopy and sun together: the directional light, the crown colours and the shade texture all read the same state.
- Planting confirms with a root pulse: two expanding rings and six roots radiate from the site (on the plan and on the ground).
- The map to species transition is an iris: it closes on the canopy, the camera jumps to a branch and a leaf far from the city, and it opens again.
- Birds appear in proportion to canopy cover and are hidden in the species view.
- Ambient motion (specimen turn, birds) stops under Pause Motion and reduced motion.
