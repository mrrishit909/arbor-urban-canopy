# Color system: living city

| Token | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Night Soil | #161B16 | Page, panels, soil under the paving |
| Canopy | #285C3D | Evergreen crowns, primary rule, calendar bars |
| Young Leaf | #7EB35A | Primary actions, lawn, summer leaves |
| Pollen Gold | #D5B557 | Roots, planting sites, selection, autumn leaves, mid heat |
| Sky | #ADDCE2 | Sky, the cool end of the heat ramp |
| Concrete | #B7B5AC | Sidewalks, buildings, oak in the analytics layer |
| Flower Accent | #E98270 | Blossom, maple in the analytics layer, the hot end of the heat ramp |

The heat ramp runs Sky to Pollen Gold to Flower Accent; the city starts desaturated and takes on these colours as growth reaches it.
